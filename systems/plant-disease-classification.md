## The problem

Crop disease identification depends on access to extension expertise,
which is scarce in rural areas. Image-based classification offers a
route to earlier identification and treatment.

## The solution

A convolutional neural network classifying plant leaf images by disease
class, trained on the PlantVillage dataset.

**Result:** 93.74% accuracy on the test set.

## My role

Dataset preparation, model design and training, evaluation, and
documentation.
